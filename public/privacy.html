Privacy Policy

Movie Poll uses Google sign-in and Supabase to provide its polling features.

Information we collect

When you sign in with Google, we collect your Google account ID, display name, and email address. We use this information to sign you in and enforce one vote per person for each movie option. The display name is shown publicly on polls you create; your email address is never shown publicly.

Storage and sharing

This information is stored in Supabase. We do not sell or share it with others.

Deletion requests

You may request deletion of your account data by emailing rhlmraj@gmail.com.
